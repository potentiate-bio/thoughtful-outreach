# Rubric — Northwind Bioanalysis (fictional example)

> A complete, invented example so you can see what a filled-in rubric looks like. Northwind
> Bioanalysis, the Atlantic Regulated Bioanalysis Workshop, the National Drug Review Agency and
> every company and person in the demo data are fictional.

## What we sell (one sentence)

Regulated bioanalysis for clinical trials: pharmacokinetic (PK) assays by LC-MS/MS and ligand
binding, immunogenicity (ADA and neutralising-antibody) testing, and fit-for-purpose biomarker
assays — method development, validation and sample analysis.

## Who buys it

- **Sponsors** running clinical trials without their own regulated lab: bioanalytical/DMPK leads,
  clinical pharmacologists, immunogenicity leads and clinical outsourcing managers.
- **Full-service clinical CROs** that subcontract bioanalysis on their sponsors' behalf: laboratory
  vendor managers and specialty-lab directors. They look like competitors on an attendee list but
  they're buyers.
- **Large pharma** with in-house labs sometimes sends overflow and niche assays out through a
  preferred-vendor list — worth knowing, but slow to qualify, so they sit in B.

## Tiers

| Tier | Name | Means | Rules |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Direct fit | Could buy now | Clinical-stage sponsors (Ph1–Ph3) with no in-house regulated lab; clinical CROs that subcontract bioanalysis |
| B | Plausible | Could buy, something uncertain | Preclinical sponsors within ~12 months of an IND; sponsors with their own regulated lab; large pharma (preferred-vendor lists, long qualification); academic medical centers running investigator-initiated trials |
| C | Partner / referral | Won't buy, can refer or co-market | Central labs (safety testing, kits, logistics); biomarker/diagnostics companies; clinical pharmacology and regulatory consultancies; instrument and reagent vendors |
| X | Out of scope | Not a fit now | Competing bioanalytical CROs; regulators and standards bodies; investors; discovery-only and research-tools companies; animal health |

### Priority within A

1 = clinical-stage sponsors (one study, one decision-maker, outsource everything regulated).
2 = clinical CROs (bigger volume across many sponsors, but a vendor-qualification process first).

## Hard exclusions

- Competitors: Plinthwell Analytical, Ferncastle Bioanalytics, Kittering Analytical, Matrix & Method Labs.
- Regulators and standards bodies: relationship only — never a sales conversation.
- Discovery-only companies with no clinical or IND-enabling program.
- Animal health.

## Spotlight

- Theme: **immunogenicity** — our strongest, hardest-to-replace service.
- `strong`: the company's clinical pipeline is mainly biologics or oligos that need ADA testing
- `partial`: at least one clinical program needs ADA testing

### Preselect rule (differs by segment)
- **Clinical-stage sponsors:** seniority in {Exec / VP+, Director, Senior IC} and function in
  {Bioanalytical / DMPK, Clinical pharmacology, Immunogenicity, Biomarker / translational,
  Clinical outsourcing}; plus Clinical operations and Leadership at Director and above.
- **Clinical CROs:** Director and above in Clinical outsourcing, Lab operations or Clinical operations.
- **Spotlight `strong`, any tier:** seniority in {Exec / VP+, Director, Senior IC}, excluding BD and Other.

## Event profiles

### Atlantic Regulated Bioanalysis Workshop
A scientific workshop: heavy with competitor scientists, in-house pharma bioanalysts and regulators.
- In-house pharma lab scientists are **peers here, not buyers** — the reason large pharma stays in
  B at this event. Talk shop; don't pitch.
- Regulators: tag `Regulator: listen, don't pitch`; never in the shortlist.
- Timing: request meetings in the workshop app before day 1; posters are the best place for
  unplanned conversations with sponsor scientists.
- Central labs exhibiting here are pitching specialty bioanalysis → X for this event *(added
  after the 2027 run — see changelog)*.
