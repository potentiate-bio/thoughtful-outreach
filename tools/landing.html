Open-source AI skill

Find the right people on any list. Then write to them yourself.

Score a conference attendee list (or any prospect list) against your ideal customer profile, research only the people you pick, and turn your disagreements into a sharper ICP. It never writes the outreach.

Try the target map See a research briefing Get the skill Read why I built it
  1. ScoreEvery name gets a tier, a segment, signals and a one-line reason, so nobody is skipped because you ran out of patience at row 400.
  2. Review & pickFilter, read the reasons, override what's wrong (with a why), and tick the people worth your time.
  3. ResearchTwo verified highlights per person, a confidence level, agenda and CRM cross-checks. “Unsure” is an allowed answer.
  4. RefineYour overrides become proposed rubric changes, for this event only or for your core ICP. You accept or reject each.

Principles

  • You own the ICP. If you can't describe it, the AI can't apply it.
  • Research only. The machine finds the signal; what you say, how and when stays yours.
  • Unsure is an answer. No guessed facts, no guessed email addresses by default.
  • Everything is auditable. Every tier has a reason; every highlight has a source.

Works with

Claude, ChatGPT / Codex, Gemini, Cursor and other tools that support the open Agent Skills format, plus a paste-in prompt for any AI chat. Setup instructions are in the repository README.

The demo uses an entirely fictional vendor, event, companies and people. Lists you load into these pages never leave your browser; this site only counts visits with Google Analytics.
