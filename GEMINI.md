# Gemini instructions

This repository contains skills in the open Agent Skills format. Each skill is a folder under
`skills/` with a `SKILL.md` describing when and how to use it.

| Skill | Use when the user… |
|---|---|
| [`target-list-research`](skills/target-list-research/SKILL.md) | shares a conference attendee list, prospect list or CRM export and wants to know who to meet; asks to score or tier a list against their ICP; asks to research a shortlist; or asks to refine their ICP from overrides |

If your agent doesn't load skills automatically, read the matching `SKILL.md` in full before
starting, and follow the files it references by relative path.

Standing rule for every skill here: **research only — never draft outreach messages.**
